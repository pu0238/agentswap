AgentSwap · demo storyboard v2.1

This video tells hackathon judges and AI-agent devs that AgentSwap lets any AI agent swap Solana tokens in one x402-paid HTTP call, without touching its keys. It uses the approved navy system: square 2px frames, Archivo Expanded, holo on deep panels, and the 3D caltrop (drawn here as a flat stand-in).
